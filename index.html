Offsite Backup

Keep your data safe, somewhere else.

Why offsite backup?

Fire, theft, hardware failure and ransomware can destroy a backup that lives next to your original data. An offsite backup keeps a separate copy in a different physical location, so you can always recover.

What you get

  • Automatic daily backups of files, servers and databases.
  • End-to-end encryption: only you hold the keys.
  • Data stored in geographically separate data centers.
  • Versioned history, so you can restore earlier copies of a file.
  • Simple, fast restores when you need them.

How it works

  1. Choose what to protect.
  2. Set a schedule.
  3. We encrypt and copy your data offsite.
  4. Restore any time, to any machine.
